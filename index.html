About Us

A grassroots NGO focused on dignity, learning, sustainability, and service.

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organization built around hands-on community service. Our teams plan local drives, train young volunteers, collaborate with partners, and keep every initiative centered on dignity, inclusion, and measurable support for people who need it most.

Vision

A society where every child can learn, every family can access support, and every young person can contribute.

Mission

Convert compassion into organized field action through volunteering, internships, and community partnerships.

Organizational values

  • Integrity in every initiative
  • Compassion for people and animals
  • Sustainability as a shared responsibility
  • Learning-led youth empowerment

Programs and Initiatives

Programs that meet urgent needs and build long-term hope.

Food Distribution Program

Meal and ration drives for children, daily wage workers, senior citizens, and families facing hardship.

Tree Plantation Drives

Plantation, sapling care, cleanliness, and awareness drives that make sustainability local and visible.

Animal Welfare Activities

Feeding, rescue coordination, treatment support, adoption awareness, and care for animals in distress.

Education Support Programs

Study kits, mentoring, life-skills sessions, digital access support, and encouragement for young learners.

Community Development Projects

Health awareness, hygiene drives, local outreach, and neighborhood activities planned with residents.

Internship Opportunities

Structured internships that train youth in leadership, documentation, outreach, teamwork, and service.
